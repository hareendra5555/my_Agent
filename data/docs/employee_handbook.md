# Alderly Robotics Employee Handbook

## Leave Policy

### Parental Leave

Full-time employees are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave after
one year of continuous employment. Leave can be taken continuously or split
into two blocks within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption.

### Sick Leave

Employees accrue 1 day of paid sick leave per month, up to a maximum of 12
days per calendar year. Unused sick leave does not roll over to the next
year.

### Vacation Policy

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid vacation per year during their
first three years, increasing to 20 days per year after three years of
tenure. Vacation requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance
through the HR portal.

## Expense Policy

All business expenses over $75 require a manager's written approval and a
submitted receipt within 30 days of purchase. Expenses under $75 can be
self-approved but still require a receipt for reimbursement. Alcohol is
reimbursable only for client-facing meals, up to $50 per person.

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed every
6 months.

## IT & Security Policy

### Password Requirements

All company accounts require a minimum 14-character password and mandatory
two-factor authentication. Passwords must be rotated every 180 days.

### Device Policy

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and are re-imaged
every 24 months. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT Security
within 1 hour of discovery.

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in March and September.
Ratings use a 4-point scale: Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations,
Partially Meets Expectations, and Below Expectations. Compensation changes
tied to reviews take effect the following pay cycle.
