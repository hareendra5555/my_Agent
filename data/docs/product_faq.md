# Alderly Robotics Product FAQ

## Pricing Tiers

### Starter Plan

The Starter plan costs $29 per month and includes up to 3 robot units, 1,000
API calls per day, and email-only support with a 48-hour response time.

### Growth Plan

The Growth plan costs $99 per month and includes up to 15 robot units,
10,000 API calls per day, and email/chat support with a 24-hour response
time.

### Enterprise Plan

The Enterprise plan is custom-priced, includes unlimited robot units, a
negotiable API rate limit, a dedicated account manager, and a 1-hour SLA for
critical support tickets.

## Refund Policy

Customers can request a full refund within 14 days of the initial purchase
if fewer than 100 API calls have been made on the account. After 14 days,
refunds are prorated based on unused months remaining on an annual plan; no
refunds are issued on monthly plans after the 14-day window.

## Warranty

All robot hardware units carry a 2-year manufacturer warranty covering
defects in materials and workmanship. The warranty does not cover damage
from unauthorized modification, water damage, or drops from a height
greater than 1 meter.

## Firmware Updates

Firmware updates are pushed automatically over Wi-Fi and cannot be disabled
for security-critical patches. Feature updates can be deferred for up to 90
days through the admin dashboard.

## Cancellation Policy

Subscriptions can be canceled anytime from the billing dashboard and remain
active until the end of the current billing cycle. There is no early
termination fee on monthly plans; annual plans canceled early forfeit the
remaining prepaid balance.
